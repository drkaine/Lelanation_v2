Statistic = base + bonus + g × (n − 1) × (0.7025 + 0.0175 × (n − 1))

Stat Increase = g × (0.65 + 0.035 × n)

When leveling up from level 1 to level 2, the champion gains 0.65 + 0.035 × 2 = 0.72 = 72% of the growth stat. When leveling up from level 9 to level 10, the champion gains 0.65 + 0.035 × 10 = 1.00 = 100% of the growth stat. When leveling up from level 17 to level 18, the champion gains 0.65 + 0.035 × 18 = 1.28 = 128% of the growth stat.

By level 18, a champion will have gained a total of 1700%, or 17 times, their growth statistic.
